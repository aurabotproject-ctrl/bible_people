# Bible Guess Who — Content Bible (Part 2 of 2: Days 101-200)

**New Testament Edition** — Days 101-200 cover the New Testament, continuing directly from Part 1 (Days 1-100, Old Testament) to complete the full 200-day game.

---

## How This Document Is Organized

Each day has:
- **Book(s):** where their story is found, for reference
- **Clue 1 / Clue 2 / Clue 3:** first-person riddle clues, shown one at a time. Clue 1 is deliberately hard and cryptic (no proper nouns, no obvious giveaways), Clue 2 is moderately harder still but starting to narrow things down, and Clue 3 is a clear, confident identification. The player guesses after each clue; if they haven't guessed correctly by the end of Clue 3, reveal the answer automatically.
- **Answer:** the character's name (what the dropdown/day should be labeled)
- **Fun Facts:** 3 short, punchy, kid-friendly facts
- **Their Story:** a short paragraph (written for ages 8-12) explaining who they are and why they matter
- **Image Prompt:** a ready-to-use prompt for an AI image generator, written to work with the Global Image Style Guide below

**Note on ordering:** Days are intentionally NOT in biblical order — Gospels, Acts, and Epistles figures are mixed throughout, and famous names sit alongside more obscure ones, so students can't guess the answer just from the sequence.

## Global Image Style Guide

Use this as the base style for every character portrait, then add the character-specific scene described in that day's Image Prompt:

> "Warm, friendly children's storybook illustration, digital painting style, soft painterly textures, rich warm color palette (golds, terracottas, deep blues), gentle lighting, ancient Middle Eastern / Roman-era setting, clothing and props historically inspired but simplified, character has a kind and expressive face, no scary or violent imagery, no text or words in the image, wholesome and appropriate for children ages 8-12, single clear focal character, square 1:1 composition."

## Day 101: Nicolas

**Book(s):** Acts 6

**Clue 1:** I was a Gentile convert to Judaism before becoming one of the first deacons of the church.
**Clue 2:** I was chosen alongside Stephen and Philip to help ensure fair daily distribution of food to widows.
**Clue 3:** I came specifically from the city of Antioch, unlike most of the other men chosen with me.

**Answer:** Nicolas

**Fun Facts:**
- I was a proselyte from Antioch, meaning a Gentile convert to Judaism.
- I was one of the seven men chosen to serve as the first deacons in Jerusalem.
- I'm mentioned only once by name, alongside Stephen and Philip, in the book of Acts.

**Their Story:** Nicolas was a Gentile convert to Judaism from the city of Antioch, chosen alongside six other men, including Stephen and Philip, to serve as the first deacons in the Jerusalem church. Their role was to ensure fair, practical care for the community's widows so that the apostles could focus on preaching and prayer. Though Nicolas is mentioned only once by name, his selection shows the early church's commitment to including believers from diverse backgrounds in meaningful service.

**Image Prompt:** A group of seven men being commissioned by the apostles through the laying on of hands, simple gathering hall, warm communal atmosphere.

**Answer:** Bartholomew (Nathanael)

**Fun Facts:**

**Their Story:** Bartholomew, likely the same man called Nathanael in John's Gospel, was initially skeptical when his friend Philip told him about Jesus from Nazareth. Jesus praised him as a man of complete honesty, and he became one of the twelve apostles.
