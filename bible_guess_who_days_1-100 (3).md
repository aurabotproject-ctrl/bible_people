# Bible Guess Who — Content Bible (Part 1 of 2: Days 1-100)

**Old Testament Edition** — Days 1-100 cover the Old Testament. Part 2 (Days 101-200) covers the New Testament, so the full 200-day game spans the whole Bible.

---

## How This Document Is Organized

Each day has:
- **Book(s):** where their story is found, for reference
- **Clue 1 / Clue 2 / Clue 3:** first-person riddle clues, shown one at a time. Clue 1 is deliberately hard and cryptic (no proper nouns, no obvious giveaways), Clue 2 is moderately harder still but starting to narrow things down, and Clue 3 is a clear, confident identification. The player guesses after each clue; if they haven't guessed correctly by the end of Clue 3, reveal the answer automatically.
- **Answer:** the character's name (what the dropdown/day should be labeled)
- **Fun Facts:** 3 short, punchy, kid-friendly facts
- **Their Story:** a short paragraph (written for ages 8-12) explaining who they are and why they matter
- **Image Prompt:** a ready-to-use prompt for an AI image generator, written to work with the Global Image Style Guide below

**Note on ordering:** Days are intentionally NOT in biblical/chronological order. They're shuffled so students can't guess the answer just by knowing what comes next in the Bible's storyline.

## Global Image Style Guide

Use this as the base style for every character portrait, then add the character-specific scene described in that day's Image Prompt:

> "Warm, friendly children's storybook illustration, digital painting style, soft painterly textures, rich warm color palette (golds, terracottas, deep blues), gentle lighting, ancient Middle Eastern / biblical-era setting, clothing and props historically inspired but simplified, character has a kind and expressive face, no scary or violent imagery, no text or words in the image, wholesome and appropriate for children ages 8-12, single clear focal character, square 1:1 composition."

## Day 1: Boaz

**Book(s):** Ruth 2–4

**Clue 1:** I noticed a hardworking stranger gleaning in my fields long before I ever spoke to her.
**Clue 2:** I settled a matter of family duty at the city gate in front of witnesses.
**Clue 3:** I married Ruth and became an ancestor of King David.

**Answer:** Boaz

**Fun Facts:**
- I was a wealthy landowner known for my kindness to workers.
- I made sure Ruth had extra grain to collect by instructing my workers to leave some behind on purpose.
- I'm mentioned in the family tree of Jesus in the New Testament.

**Their Story:** Boaz was a respected and wealthy landowner who noticed Ruth's hard work and loyalty to her mother-in-law Naomi while she gathered leftover grain in his fields. Moved by her character, he made sure she was protected and had more than enough to gather. Their relationship grew into marriage, and their family line eventually led to King David — and generations later, to Jesus. Boaz's kindness is remembered as a model of generosity and integrity.

**Image Prompt:** A respected landowner standing at the edge of a golden wheat field speaking kindly to workers, warm daylight, fields stretching to the horizon.

## Day 2: Naomi

**Book(s):** Ruth 1–4

**Clue 1:** I told people to call me by a name meaning 'bitter' after everything I'd lost.
**Clue 2:** I urged both my daughters-in-law to go back home, but only one refused to leave me.
**Clue 3:** I was Ruth's mother-in-law, and Boaz was my close relative.

**Answer:** Naomi

**Fun Facts:**
- My name means "pleasant," though I once asked people to call me "Mara," meaning "bitter."
- My daughter-in-law Ruth famously refused to leave me, even after we lost everything.
- I became the great-great-grandmother of King David through Ruth's marriage to Boaz.

**Their Story:** Naomi faced tremendous loss, losing her husband and both of her sons while living far from her homeland. Deciding to return home, she encouraged her daughters-in-law to start new lives — but Ruth refused to leave her side, offering one of the most touching declarations of loyalty in the Bible. Naomi went on to help guide Ruth toward a new marriage with Boaz, turning a story that began in deep sorrow into one of restoration and hope.

**Image Prompt:** An older woman walking along a dusty road with a younger woman beside her, both carrying small travel bundles, golden hills stretching into the distance, a bittersweet but hopeful mood.

**Answer:** Nehemiah

**Fun Facts:**

Remarkably, the massive project was completed in just 52 days, and Nehemiah's leadership helped restore both the city and the people's confidence.

**Clue 3:** I was a Syrian commander healed of leprosy after washing in the Jordan River.

**Answer:** Naaman

**Fun Facts:**
